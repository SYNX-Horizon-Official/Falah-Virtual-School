Our Approach

Knowledge that shapes character

Islamic education should be more than memorising information. Children should understand what they learn, reflect on its meaning and gradually bring Islamic values into the way they think, speak and act.

Quran & Seerah

Growing with the Quran & Seerah

At Falah Virtual School, Quranic learning is centred on understanding, reflection and meaningful application rather than rote learning alone. Students explore age-appropriate Surahs, their translation and meaning, and develop a deeper appreciation of why these teachings matter in everyday life. They also learn from the Seerah of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) as a practical example of character, compassion, responsibility and faith in action.

Through progressive learning, daily duas, the beautiful names and attributes of Allah, and practical Islamic guidance, children are encouraged to build a thoughtful and lasting connection with their faith.

Our Curriculum

Faith connected with everyday learning

Islamic knowledge is not treated as something separate from the rest of a child’s development. Quranic understanding, Tawheed, the names of Allah, the pillars of Islam, Seerah, duas and Islamic manners contribute to the wider journey of developing knowledge, character and confidence.

Children are encouraged to connect what they learn with their everyday choices, relationships, responsibilities and behaviour.

Character & Manners

Islamic values in everyday life

Character grows through repeated practice. Children are encouraged to recognise how faith shapes everyday behaviour, relationships and responsibility.
