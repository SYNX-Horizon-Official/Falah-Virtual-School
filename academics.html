Progression

From foundations to independence

Students progress through a connected learning journey, moving from strong foundations toward deeper understanding, application, independence and future-ready capability.

Stage 1 — Foundation KG – Grade 1

Literacy, numeracy, early science, Islamic learning and positive learning habits.

Stage 2 — Development Grades 2 – 3

Reading fluency, writing, mathematical reasoning, investigation, projects and technology.

Stage 3 — Application Grades 4 – 5

Advanced literacy, research, presentation, coding and interdisciplinary projects.

Stage 4 — Independence Grades 6 – 7

Critical thinking, independent research, AI awareness, leadership and project management.

Learning That Connects

Knowledge becomes more powerful when children can use it

At Falah, academic learning is connected with communication, projects, technology, values and real-world application. Students are encouraged to ask questions, explain their ideas, work through challenges and reflect on what they discover.

This connected approach helps children move beyond simply knowing information toward becoming curious, confident, capable and responsible learners.
